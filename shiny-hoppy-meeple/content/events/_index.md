We meet **every Thursday, 7:30–11pm** at
[Boundary Taproom](https://www.instagram.com/boundarytaproom/) in East Belfast. Just turn up — grab a
drink and we'll find a game that suits you. Never played with us before? That's genuinely fine, and
you don't need to bring anything.

The calendar below covers the next few months, including the occasional **Saturday session**. For
announcements between sessions, follow
[@shinyhoppymeepleni](https://www.instagram.com/shinyhoppymeepleni/) — and if you'd like to pick
something out in advance, have a browse through [the games we bring](/library/).
